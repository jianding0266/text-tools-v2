About TextTools Online

TextTools Online is a free collection of text processing tools launched in 2026. We built this platform because we believe that useful tools should be accessible to everyone — without registration fees, without data collection, and without complicated interfaces.

Our tools serve a wide range of users. Students use our word counter to meet essay requirements. Writers use our case converter to format titles and headings. Developers use our diff checker and text replacer during code reviews. Content creators use our markdown preview to draft blog posts. And anyone who works with lists uses our sorter to organize their data quickly.

Our Privacy Promise

Privacy is not an afterthought at TextTools — it is a core design principle. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. When you enter text, process it, or copy the result, nothing is ever sent to our servers. We do not log your activity, track your behavior, or store any of your content. Your text is yours, and it stays on your device.

We fund the project through non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense. These ads do not collect your personal data or analyze your text. We do not use analytics cookies or any form of behavioral tracking.
