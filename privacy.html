Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At TextTools, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

Data We Do Not Collect

Text content: We do not collect, store, or transmit any text you enter into our tools. All processing happens entirely within your browser. Your content never leaves your device.

Personal information: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information through our tools.

Cookies: We do not set any tracking cookies. The only cookies that may be present are those set by Google AdSense for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. Google's use of advertising cookies is subject to their Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Children's Privacy

TextTools is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@quiktexttools.top.
