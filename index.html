Why Use TextTools Online?

TextTools Online is a free collection of text processing tools designed for writers, students, developers, and anyone who works with text on a daily basis. Whether you need to count the words in an essay, convert text to a different case, compare two documents, or organize a list of items, our tools handle it quickly and securely.

Privacy by design: Unlike many online tools that send your text to a server, every operation in TextTools runs entirely in your browser. Your content never leaves your device — no cookies tracking your writing, no data collection, no accounts to create. We believe your text is yours, and it should stay that way.

No registration required: All tools are available instantly. There is no sign-up wall, no free trial that expires, and no premium tier that locks basic features behind a paywall. Everything is free, forever.

What Can You Do With TextTools?

Word Counter — Essential for students and writers. Get accurate counts of words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs. It also estimates reading time and shows your top 10 most frequent words, helping you analyze writing patterns and improve clarity.

Case Converter — Instantly transform text between six formats: lowercase, uppercase, title case (capitalize the first letter of each word), sentence case (capitalize the first letter of each sentence), alternating case, and reverse case. Useful for formatting, coding, and content creation.

Text Diff Checker — Paste two versions of a document and see exactly what changed. Lines are color-coded: green for additions, red for removals. Great for code reviews, editing tracking, and comparing revisions.

Markdown Preview — Write in Markdown format and see a rendered preview in real time. Supports headings, bold, italics, lists, links, code blocks, and tables. Perfect for bloggers, documentation writers, and GitHub users.

Text Replacer — Find and replace text efficiently. Supports literal string matching and regular expressions. Toggle case sensitivity for precise control. Useful for batch text editing, data cleaning, and formatting automation.

List Sorter — Organize any list of text items. Sort alphabetically (A-Z or Z-A), by length (shortest or longest first), randomly, in reverse order, or remove duplicates. Perfect for organizing contact lists, shopping lists, or any grouped data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TextTools really free?

Yes. TextTools is and always will be completely free. We support the project through non-intrusive advertisements. There are no hidden fees, no premium upgrades, and no limits on tool usage.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All text processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is uploaded to any server. We do not collect, store, or share any of your content. Your privacy is our top priority.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use all tools immediately without signing up, logging in, or providing any personal information. Just open the tool and start using it.

Can I use TextTools on my phone?

Yes. TextTools is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. The interface adapts to any screen size.
