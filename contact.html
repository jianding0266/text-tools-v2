Frequently Asked Questions

Is TextTools free?

Yes. All tools on TextTools are completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no usage limits.

Do you collect my data?

No. All text processing happens locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your content.

How can I request a new tool?

Use the contact form above or email us directly. We review all suggestions and add new tools based on user demand.

Can I use TextTools for commercial projects?

Yes. There are no restrictions on how you use the tools. Use them for personal, educational, or commercial purposes.
